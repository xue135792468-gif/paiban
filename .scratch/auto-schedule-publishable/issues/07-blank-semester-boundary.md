# 票据 07: 空白学期从零搜索时给出可见提示

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement
Priority: P3
**Spec:** ../spec.md
**来源:** 2026-10-31 code-review Spec 残余项（拆分自原票 06）

## What

既没有已发布基线、也没有完整草稿的学期没有 warm start，只能从零搜索；60/300 秒都可能停在少量冲突上。`spec.md` 已写明这个边界是 best effort，但排课页面上没有任何提示，用户会以为系统“应该”一次排到 0。

## Why

有基线/导入草稿的学期已保证 0 硬冲突；空白学期是唯一不能承诺的场景。把边界放在用户操作的地方，比只写在 spec/ADR 里更有用，也能避免“为什么这次没排好”的误解。

## Acceptance

- [ ] 启动排课任务时，系统能区分并记录 warm start 来源：已发布基线 / 完整草稿 / 无。
- [ ] 来源为「无」时，排课页的任务卡片显示信息性提示：该学期没有可用的已发布课表或完整导入草稿，本次从零搜索，可能仍需人工调整；可加长求解时间或先导入草稿。
- [ ] 提示不阻塞提交、不改变求解行为、不伪装成发布拦截。
- [ ] 空白学期的集成测试：启动任务 → 断言来源为「无」且提示可见 → 记录实际结果；不承诺 0 硬冲突。
- [ ] 有基线或完整草稿的学期不显示该提示。

### Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Show where the warm start came from and warn when a blank semester has to search from scratch.

**Current behavior:**
A blank semester (no published baseline, no complete draft) starts a from-scratch search with no indication in the scheduling page. Users cannot tell why this run may need more time or manual adjustment.

**Desired behavior:**
The scheduling job exposes its warm-start source: published baseline, complete draft, or none. When it is none, the job card on the scheduling page shows a non-blocking informational warning with the text above. Jobs with a baseline or complete draft show no warning. The solver behavior and the publish blockers are unchanged.

**Key interfaces:**
- The scheduling job DTO gains a warm-start-source field (or an equivalent explicit flag) populated by the problem builder/seeding path.
- The scheduling page reads that field and renders the warning only for the "none" value.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A job started in a semester with a published baseline reports that source and shows no warning.
- [ ] A job started in a semester with a complete draft reports that source and shows no warning.
- [ ] A job started in a blank semester reports "none" and shows the warning; the warning is informational only.
- [ ] An integration test covers the blank-semester path and records the produced score without asserting 0 hard.
- [ ] Existing scheduling tests and the scheduling e2e spec still pass.

**Out of scope:**
- Making a blank-semester run guarantee 0 hard conflicts.
- Auto-importing a workbook or auto-creating a baseline.
- Changing the solver configuration or the warm-start policy.
